# Examples

Here is something we've put together so you could feel more comfortable with the `py-ibm-4610` library.

## 1. Simple receipt

We've put together an example of a receipt from a made up place just to showcase a kind of real life example on how you can use `py-ibm-4610` python library to print a receipt.

Source: [simple_receipt.py](/examples/simple_receipt.py)

## 2. QR Code

You can print out QR Codes on your IBM/Toshiba 4610 receipt printer (within certain firmware version restrictions, though).

## 3. Printer's ringtone

Some of the 4610 models are equipped with a beeper. It can do beeps of various frequencies and duration. There is most certainly a reason for a beeper to exist in a POS printer, but you can't ignore the opportunity to have some fun with it.

Source: [play_melody.py](/examples/play_melody.py)
